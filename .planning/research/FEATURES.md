# Features Research for BSR Shopping Mall

## Table Stakes (Must Haves)
- **Product Catalog & Details**: High-quality imagery, pricing, size charts, and stock status.
- **Search & Filtering**: Ability to filter by Men, Women, Kids, and specific brands.
- **Shopping Cart & Checkout**: Frictionless cart management and a fast 3-step checkout process.
- **Payment Gateway Integration**: UPI (PhonePe, GPay), Credit/Debit, Net Banking, COD via Razorpay.
- **Responsive Design**: Must be perfectly usable on mobile devices.
- **Contact & Location**: Click-to-call, WhatsApp integration, and Google Maps embed.

## Differentiators
- **All India Delivery Tracking**: Native UI for tracking shipped orders directly on the website.
- **Bulk Order Portal**: A dedicated inquiry workflow for festival/school uniform orders, which is a unique B2B angle for a standard retail store.
- **Multi-Language Support**: English + Telugu toggle for localized reach.

## Anti-features (Do NOT Build)
- **Native iOS/Android Apps**: Out of scope for this budget and timeline. The mobile web experience must be sufficient.
- **Complex Loyalty Programs**: Defer to post-launch; focus on core conversion first.
