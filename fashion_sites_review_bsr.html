PL
Pantaloons
Mid-market family retail · Aditya Birla Group · Brick + click
A large-format Indian fashion retailer targeting middle-income families. Operates 450+ physical stores across India with a full online storefront. Covers women, men, kids, footwear, accessories and fragrances. Runs a loyalty programme (GreenCard) and gift cards. Strongest in ethnic + western wear for women and kids. Price range: ₹300–₹3,000.
✅ What they do well
Top utility bar — GreenCard, Gift Card, Store Locator, Track Order, Contact — all visible before the fold
Shop by Occasion filter (Party wear, Wedding wear, Festive wear, Work wear) — extremely useful for Indian shoppers
Shop by Brand section under each gender — builds trust and makes browsing faster
Store Mode toggle — shows what's physically available in a nearby store vs online only
Deep subcategory nav — prevents dead-end browsing, customers find exactly what they want
⚠️ Weak spots
Homepage feels cluttered — too many banners competing for attention
No WhatsApp integration — misses India's largest customer communication channel
Mobile nav is heavy and slow to load
Loyalty programme Shop by occasion Store locator Track order Gift cards
NV
Nuvrah
Luxury designer multi-brand · Shopify · Hyderabad
A Hyderabad-based premium multi-brand fashion store carrying 40+ Indian designers including Manish Malhotra, Gaurav Gupta, Raghavendra Rathore and Anushree Reddy. Physical store + online. 752 products across men, women, accessories and jewellery. Price range: ₹5,000–₹5,89,999. Has a private shopping service and a café inside the store. Strong branding and editorial imagery.
✅ What they do well
Professional campaign-quality photography — every product shot is editorial grade
Designer filter on collection page — lets customers browse by their favourite label
Private Shopping page — builds exclusivity and premium positioning
New Arrivals section per gender — keeps returning customers engaged with fresh stock
WebP images served via Shopify CDN at 4K source resolution — crisp on all screens
Discover Nuvrah section (Café, About Us, Locate Us) — humanises the brand beyond a product catalogue
⚠️ Weak spots
No customer reviews visible on product cards — missing for a ₹10k+ price point
Subcategory tags in filters are inconsistent (e.g. "kurta" and "Kurta" listed as separate items)
No WhatsApp CTA despite being a premium Indian brand
Designer filter New arrivals Private shopping About us story Café / experience

Nykaa Fashion

MY
Myntra
India's largest fashion marketplace · Flipkart Group
India's dominant fashion e-commerce platform. 6,000+ brands, 1M+ products. Covers fashion, beauty, home and lifestyle. Known for M-Now (2-hour delivery in select cities), Studio (video try-on content), and Style Cast (AI outfit recommendations). Has a loyalty tier (Myntra Insider) and a strong sale event culture (EORS — End of Reason Sale). Price range: ₹199–₹1,00,000+.
✅ What they do well
Scrolling offer ticker strip below nav — shows active sale, free delivery threshold, and return policy at all times
Product cards show brand name + product type + price + discount % — complete info without clicking
Wishlist with one-tap heart icon on every product card — reduces friction
Size selector directly on collection page — no need to open product detail
Studio section — influencer/video content styled as editorial, drives aspiration
Insider loyalty badge shown on account — makes membership feel earned and visible
Recently viewed section — keeps bounce rate low, pulls users back into browsing
⚠️ Weak spots
Site blocked bot fetch — aggressive bot protection hurts SEO and scraping tools
Homepage is overwhelming for first-time visitors — too many simultaneous promotions
Offer ticker strip Wishlist on card Recently viewed Loyalty insider Studio content Size on collection page
What BSR should steal — prioritised
🔴 High priority
📣 Scrolling offer ticker
From: Myntra
A thin strip below the nav showing free delivery threshold, WhatsApp number, and store hours. Persistent across all pages. One line of CSS + JS. Maximum visibility, zero clutter.
🔴 High priority
🛍️ Shop by occasion
From: Pantaloons
Add "Wedding", "Festival / Ugadi", "Casual", "Office" filter tags. AP/Telangana shoppers think in occasions, not categories. This directly increases conversion.
🔴 High priority
🖼️ Image category tiles
From: Nykaa Fashion
Replace text-only category nav with visual circular/square image tiles — Men, Women, Kids, Bulk. One image per tile, loaded at 150px. Instantly scannable on mobile.
🟡 Medium priority
🆕 New arrivals section
From: Nuvrah
A dedicated "New this week" section on the homepage. Gives repeat visitors a reason to come back. Update weekly — even 5–10 new products is enough.
🟡 Medium priority
❤️ Wishlist on product cards
From: Myntra
A heart icon on each product card. Reduces abandonment — customers save items without adding to cart. Simple to implement in Next.js with localStorage or user account.
🟡 Medium priority
📍 Store locator + hours
From: Pantaloons
A dedicated store page with Sompeta and Palasa addresses, Google Maps embed, phone number, and opening hours. Pantaloons puts this in the top utility bar. Should be 2 clicks from anywhere.
🟡 Medium priority
🕐 Recently viewed
From: Myntra
Show the last 4–6 products a visitor browsed at the bottom of every page. Keeps them in the funnel when they navigate away. Pure frontend — no backend needed, uses localStorage.
🟢 Nice to have
📖 Our story section
From: Nuvrah (Discover Nuvrah)
A short "About BSR" page — 20 years, family-owned, Sompeta & Palasa community. Builds emotional trust. One page, 200 words, one good photo of the store. Hugely underrated for local brands.
🟢 Nice to have
🎁 Gift card or voucher
From: Pantaloons
A WhatsApp-shareable gift voucher (even a simple PDF with a code) for festivals. Easy to implement, drives gifting purchases — especially relevant for wedding season and Ugadi.
The 3 most impactful changes for BSR right now — in order: (1) offer ticker strip, (2) shop by occasion, (3) image category tiles. All three can be done in under a week with minimal dev work.
